# SCSS plug-in changelog

Every release of the SCSS plug-in (`plugins/scss`), newest first; the format is in
[../README.md](../README.md).

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-19

### Added

- The first release, for `*.scss` files, from the Marketplace in every IDE: highlighting of
  selectors, properties, values, `$variables`, `#{}` interpolation, placeholders, mixins and the
  Sass at-rules, with `//` comments; folding of blocks and comments; the variables, mixins,
  functions and rules in the Structure view; completion of properties, values, at-rules, the
  file's variables after `$` and its mixins after `@include`; colour previews in the gutter.
