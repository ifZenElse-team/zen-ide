# Images

PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP, BMP and ICO files open in an image viewer instead of the text editor. SVG
files open as text with their picture beside it ([Text with a preview](02-editor.md#text-with-a-preview)).

- The image is shown at its own size, or smaller to fit the window, over a chessboard where it is
  transparent. Its size, format, colour depth and file size are in the toolbar.
- **Zoom In** ⌘=, **Zoom Out** ⌘−, **Actual Size** ⌘/, and **Fit Zoom to Window**. ⌘ with the
  scroll wheel, or a pinch on the trackpad, zooms too.
- A picture larger than the window scrolls, and can be dragged with the mouse.
