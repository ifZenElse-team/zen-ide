# ADR-NNNN: Title in the imperative or as a noun phrase

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [ADR-NNNN](NNNN-title.md)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What problem are we solving, and what forces are at play? State facts, constraints and the
options that were on the table. Someone reading this in three years must understand why the
question mattered.

## Decision

What we decided, in one or two paragraphs, stated so that it can be checked against the code.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what we now have to do because of this decision.

## Alternatives considered

Each serious alternative and the reason it was not chosen.
